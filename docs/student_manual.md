# Student Manual

## Sign in

Sign in with the student account registered for the CUET DSW platform. The role stored in Firestore must be `student`.

## DSW Service Center

Use **DSW Service Center** for the main business workflow. Supported request categories are:

- Hall-related request
- Financial assistance request
- Student welfare complaint
- Event permission
- Certificate / recommendation request
- General grievance

### Submit a request

1. Open **DSW Service Center**.
2. Choose the service category.
3. Enter a clear title and detailed description.
4. Upload supporting PDF, JPG, or PNG files when relevant.
5. Submit the request.
6. Save the generated tracking number.

The official workflow is:

`Submitted → Under Review → Assigned → Resolved`

AI triage can suggest a priority, responsible office, summary, duplicate warning, and visible image evidence. These suggestions are advisory only and do not change the official status.

## Track requests

Open **Track Service Requests** to see:

- tracking number
- current status
- assigned officer
- official resolution note
- uploaded documents
- advisory AI analysis
- complete application history

## Notifications

The in-app notification center records submission, assignment/status-related updates, and other workflow messages. Open a notification to return to the relevant request or complaint.

## AI DSW Helpdesk

Use **AI DSW Helpdesk** for general guidance about available services and how to use the platform. The assistant must not be treated as an official source for policy, fees, deadlines, eligibility, or administrative decisions. Confirm such matters with DSW staff.

## Complaint submission

The dedicated complaint workflow remains available for complaint-specific handling, including confidentiality, supporting evidence, AI triage, duplicate analysis, image evidence analysis, officer assignment, and resolution tracking.

## Legacy specialized services

Character Certificate and Hall Transfer screens are retained for compatibility. New service categories can use the unified DSW Service Center workflow.

## AI and privacy notice

AI output is advisory. Do not rely on AI output as an approval, rejection, diagnosis, disciplinary finding, or official DSW decision. Upload only documents or images necessary for the request.
