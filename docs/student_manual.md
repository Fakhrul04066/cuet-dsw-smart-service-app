# Student Manual

## Login

Use the student login screen to sign in with your campus credentials or the local development test account.

## Home

The home screen shows the available student services:

- Character Certificate
- Hall Transfer
- Student Complaint

## Character Certificate

1. Open the Character Certificate form.
2. Fill in required student details.
3. Add purpose and supporting data.
4. Upload required documents if available.
5. Use the AI form check button.
6. Review the advisory feedback.
7. Submit the application.
8. Keep the tracking number for follow-up.

## AI Form Check

The AI form check is advisory only. It can point out missing information, unclear wording, or possible inconsistencies. The student still confirms submission manually.

## Hall Transfer

1. Open the Hall Transfer form.
2. Enter current hall and preferred hall.
3. Provide a reason.
4. Upload supporting evidence.
5. Use AI check.
6. Submit the request.
7. Monitor the request for official updates.

## Complaint Submission

1. Choose a complaint category.
2. Enter a clear title and description.
3. Optionally mark the complaint as confidential.
4. Add supporting attachment if available.
5. Submit the complaint.
6. Review official Gemini triage suggestions.
7. Wait for assignment and status updates.

## Tracking and Notifications

Students can track application status from their request list and notifications feed. Important updates include correction requests, approval, rejection, complaint assignment, and complaint resolution.

## Approved Certificate Download

Once an official approves the certificate, the student can open or download the approved certificate from the application detail screen.

## Feedback

Feedback and manual follow-up should be used to confirm any final outcome. AI suggestions must never be treated as an official decision.
