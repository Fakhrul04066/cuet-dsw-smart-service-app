# Demo Guide

This is a 7-10 minute academic demo flow.

1. Login as student
2. Show the Home screen
3. Submit a Character Certificate
4. Demonstrate the AI form check
5. Show the tracking number and application status
6. Submit a complaint with optional confidentiality
7. Login as official/admin
8. Review the certificate request and ask for correction or approval
9. Review the complaint Gemini triage recommendation
10. Manually assign the complaint and update progress
11. Return to student view
12. Show notification and status update
13. Show approved certificate download
14. Show reports and audit logs

## Presentation notes

- Make clear that AI suggestions are advisory.
- Emphasize that human officials make final decisions.
- Show that submissions remain under manual approval and oversight.
- Keep the demo concise and focused on the three service areas.
